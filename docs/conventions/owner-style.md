# Owner style rules

The owner decides things Athena cannot, and cannot read long text. Anything shown to the owner follows
these rules. They are stored verbatim, as the owner wrote them:

> Use HTML to help me understand what we're discussing. Show me with big pictures and little words without witholding key details. Use your own taste, avoid lazy design, integrate real-world pictures/icons/visuals as needed but only selectively when it makes sense. When you do use prose/detail: Be clear, concise/terse, and avoid jargon. Explain things as if the viewer is new here, but dont use weird metaphors.

The decision briefs (`docs/design/owner-decision-briefs.md`) are the first place these rules are applied.
