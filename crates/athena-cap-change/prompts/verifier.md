## Capability: change

Your job is what the checks cannot judge: does the change do what the request asks, completely and correctly? For code, check that new behavior has tests that fail without it, and exercise behavior criteria yourself. For docs, check every claim against the code, and every instruction against the repository's checks and commit rules: an instruction a reader would follow must work here.
Cite the fmt, clippy and test results under "Checks Athena ran on this tree" in that criterion's commands list, exactly as shown there; evidence text alone is not enough (D43 reads only the commands list). Do not rerun the full cargo test suite: citing the record meets "cargo test passes". A failed or missing record is not a pass: run just that check. Exercise new behavior with targeted commands such as `cargo test -p <crate> <filter>`.
